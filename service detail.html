OUR ROOTS

Rooted in Passion for Living Giants

Founded over 15 years ago, ARVORA began with a singular conviction: mature trees are irreplaceable assets to our environment and communities. Where others see trees merely as timber or obstacles, we see complex living ecosystems requiring scientific understanding, balanced pruning, and patient stewardship.

Every climber and arborist at ARVORA undergoes rigorous ISA certification, advanced rope and aerial work training, and strict zero-impact rigging methods to protect surrounding architecture and landscape ecology.

“When we care for trees, we care for the air our children breathe, the shade of our homes, and the beauty of tomorrow.”
