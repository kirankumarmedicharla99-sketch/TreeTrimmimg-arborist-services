Frequently Asked Questions

Essential knowledge on permits, crane logistics, insurance coverage, and best seasonal windows for tree care.

While light deadwooding and clearance pruning can be conducted safely year-round, major architectural crown sculpting is ideally performed during dormant winter or late autumn. This reduces sap bleed, limits vulnerability to fungal pathogens, and offers our arborists full visibility through the leafless canopy.

Yes. Our consulting desk prepares formal ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualified (TRAQ) reports, ultrasonic tomograph scans, and heritage tree documentation required by municipal authorities and homeowner associations to secure necessary tree work permits swiftly.

We deploy composite ground-protection track mats that distribute heavy machine load evenly across sensitive turf, preventing soil compaction. For complex extractions, sections are tethered using dynamic friction ropes and hoisted over roofs and glass solariums by crane without ever touching the ground.

Standard stump grinding excavates 8 to 14 inches beneath the soil surface, fully destroying the root collar and major lateral roots. For foundation paving or new specimen tree planting, we can excavate up to 24 inches and extract the entire structural root ball upon request.

Acoustic sonic tomography is 100% non-invasive. We attach electronic sound sensors around the trunk circumference and introduce gentle sound impulses. By calculating the micro-velocity of the sound waves, our computer creates a 3D color slice revealing internal decay or hollows without puncturing or damaging the tree’s living vascular cambium.

ARVORA maintains comprehensive commercial general liability insurance up to $5,000,000 and full statutory workers' compensation for all certified crew members. Prior to commencing any high-risk extraction, certificates of insurance naming your estate are issued upon request.

OUR ROOTS

Rooted in Passion for Living Giants

Founded over 15 years ago, ARVORA began with a singular conviction: mature trees are irreplaceable assets to our environment and communities. Where others see trees merely as timber or obstacles, we see complex living ecosystems requiring scientific understanding, balanced pruning, and patient stewardship.

Every climber and arborist at ARVORA undergoes rigorous ISA certification, advanced rope and aerial work training, and strict zero-impact rigging methods to protect surrounding architecture and landscape ecology.
